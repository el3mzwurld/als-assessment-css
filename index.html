FAQs

How does URL shortening work?

URL shortening works by taking a long URL and creating a shorter, condensed version that redirects to the original URL. When a user clicks on the shortened link, they are redirected to the intended destination.

Is it necessary to create an account to use the URL shortening service?

No. You can shorten URLs without an account. Creating an account gives you access to additional features such as managing your links and viewing their performance.

Are the shortened links permanent? Will they expire?

Shortened links do not expire by default and remain available as long as the service is active and the destination is valid. Specific account or service terms may apply.

Are there any limitations on the number of URLs I can shorten?

URL limits depend on your plan. Check the pricing details for the current limits and options available to your account.

Can I customize the shortened URLs to reflect my brand or content?

Yes. You can customize the link ending, and eligible plans can use a branded domain to make links recognizable and consistent with your brand.

Can I track the performance of my shortened URLs?

Yes. Link analytics can show performance information such as the number of clicks. Available metrics and reporting features depend on your plan.

How secure is the URL shortening service? Are the shortened links protected against spam or malicious activity?

The service uses security measures to help detect and prevent abusive or harmful links. No link service can guarantee that every threat will be caught, so avoid opening links from untrusted sources.

What is a QR code and what can it do?

A QR code is a scannable image that can direct a phone or other device to a link. You can create one for a shortened URL and share it in print or digital materials.

Is there an API available for integrating the URL shortening service into my own applications or websites?

Yes. An API is available for integrating link creation and management into your applications. Review the developer documentation for access requirements and usage limits.
